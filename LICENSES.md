# Pack licenses

The code and the site in this repo are MIT ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). A voice pack is audio, and
each pack states its own license in the index (`license`, `attribution`) and in its
`pack.json`.

| Made with | License of the pack | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ElevenLabs, on a paid plan | Free to use, commercial use included | ElevenLabs grants commercial use of output made on a paid plan. The pack names the voice it used |
| Kokoro (Kokoro-82M) | Apache-2.0 | The Kokoro model and its voices are Apache-2.0. The pack's attribution names Kokoro |
| macOS `say` | Not published | Apple's voices are licensed for use on the Mac, so QuadCam does not publish a `say` pack |

A pack built from a free ElevenLabs plan is not published here.
